# Changelog

## 0.3.1

### Patch Changes

- ea212c9: Prepare the first public release with ten Wayfindr themes, logo-free artwork, concise documentation, previews, and automated Marketplace releases.

## 0.3.0

- Add six Gray themes: light and dark in Soft, Standard, and Contrast levels.
- Use neutral surfaces and muted syntax colors, buttons, and gold accents in the Gray family.
- Add stronger borders, outlined matches, and clearer indentation in Contrast variants.
- Require at least 7:1 tested text contrast and 3:1 boundary contrast in the Contrast pair.
- Preserve all four existing palettes without changes.

## 0.2.0

- Add Wayfindr Dark and Wayfindr Light as the primary pair, with muted yellow backgrounds and warm surface layers.
- Keep the original Wayfindr Night and Wayfindr Day palettes unchanged.
- Adjust the new Light palette's syntax and secondary text for contrast on yellow surfaces.
- Derive VSIX filenames from the package version.

## 0.1.0

- Introduce Wayfindr Night and Wayfindr Day.
- Theme the workbench, editor, terminal, diffs, diagnostics, and completion widgets.
- Include TextMate and semantic syntax colors.
